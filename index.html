BeautyFlow

A web platform that lets small beauty businesses in Saudi Arabia import products together. Orders are grouped to share shipping costs, packaging is designed with AI, and every shipment is tracked.

Graduation project, Al-Baha University, 2025–2026. Built with Agile iterations.

View source on GitHub
What I owned, end to end
  • Analysed and documented the functional and non-functional requirements from workflows, user roles and business rules.
  • Designed the architecture and the PostgreSQL schema for orders, users, suppliers, payments and shipping.
  • Built the Flask backend, the HTML/CSS/JavaScript front end and the AI integrations.
  • Modelled the system with UML use case, class, sequence and activity diagrams.
  • Applied security and access control, then wrote and ran the full test plan.

Collaborative shipping

Groups of up to 5 members split shipping across 20 Saudi cities. Solo shipping is also available.

AI custom packaging

A step-by-step wizard collects product, formula, finish and a description, then GPT-Image-1 generates the packaging design.

SmartPicks and Mika

Style-based product recommendations, plus a bilingual Arabic and English chatbot on GPT-4o-mini.

Transparent costs

Every invoice itemises subtotal, shipping, 5% customs, the 20 SAR SFDA fee, handling and 15% VAT.

Secure by design

SMS one-time-code two-factor sign-in with Twilio, CSRF protection and access control that returns 401 to unauthorised API calls.

Shipment tracking

A unique tracking number and five stages from Order Confirmed to Delivered, with delivery estimates of 5 to 16 days.

Tested like a product

Pages were tested one by one, then as a whole system. 53 cases across authentication, OTP, cart, AI generation, cost sharing, payments, orders and the chatbot all passed. Five defects were found and fixed.
